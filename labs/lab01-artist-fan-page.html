Michael Bublé

THE SOUND OF SWING, SOUL & SONG

A modern crooner with a classic touch, Michael Bublé brings swing-era standards and heartfelt pop songs together with warmth, humor, and a big-band sense of occasion.

THE STORY

About Michael

Michael Bublé is a Canadian singer and entertainer known for reimagining standards from the Great American Songbook and for writing contemporary pop hits. His self-titled major-label debut arrived in 2003; albums including It's Time, Crazy Love, and Christmas helped bring his polished, playful style to audiences around the world.

A few highlights

  • Born in Burnaby, British Columbia, Canada.
  • His music blends jazz and swing standards with pop, soul, and original songs.
  • His career includes Grammy and Juno Awards, plus stars on Canada's Walk of Fame and the Hollywood Walk of Fame.
  • He is known for energetic live shows and a relaxed, humorous stage presence.

Biography source: Michael Bublé's official website.

MY PERSONAL PICKS

Favorites

  1. “Home” — Its gentle melody and honest lyrics make it feel intimate, even in a big arrangement.
  2. “Feeling Good” — I love the dramatic brass and the way the performance builds with confidence.
  3. “It's Beginning to Look a Lot Like Christmas” — This version is cheerful and cozy, and it instantly sets a festive mood.
